# Fried Chicken Shift Notes

The fry station keeps these handwritten reminders and seasonal notices taped beside the timer board.

## A too-hot dial reading gets bounced back instead of rounded down

A dial reading turned well past where it belongs gets sent back for a redo, guarded against by refusing to quietly nudge it into place. A ticket written at the wrong number stays flagged until someone corrects it.

## Fry temperature schedule annual inspector certification renewal

The fry temperature schedule annual inspector certification renewal folder keeps target temp, duration, safety band, override, and stage terms filed alongside the yearly health-inspector paperwork. The front-of-house manager mails the renewal packet each spring and files the stamped copy with the other permits.

## The frying line waves off extra trays when its rack fills up

When the frying rack holds as many trays as its capacity allows, incoming trays from the coating table are turned away, guarded against by the rack's own limit rather than piling up out of sight. A turned-away tray waits at the coating table until there is room.

## Coating station dip-counter handoff notebook

The coating station dip-counter handoff notebook tracks batch, pending, capacity, queue, and rack terms for the seasoned-flour dip counter, a separate station down the line from the fryer rack itself. The dip counter keeps its own tally sheet and never reads the fryer's rack count directly.

## A too-warm soaking tub never leaves the walk-in chiller

A tub of soaking batch reading too high never leaves the walk-in fridge for the brining line to move on toward, guarded against by one plain check every single time a tub happens to be read by anyone on the crew. Only a tub reading at or under the usual mark eventually gets wheeled out toward the next step down the line.

## Marinade config validation retired salt-log binder

The marinade config validation retired salt-log binder keeps salt percent, soak minutes, brine temp, and cold-chain terms from a paper logging system retired two seasons ago when the tablets were introduced. The binder sits in the back office for historical reference only.

## Same-minute counter tickets still keep a fixed spot in line

Tickets called in the identical moment keep a fixed spot between them at the busy front counter, protected by checking a second tiebreaking mark carried on the slip rather than trusting the first called number alone. Looking at the board again some while later always finds each ticket exactly once.

## Pickup counter raffle ticket cursor tie-break drawing

The pickup counter raffle ticket cursor tie-break drawing reuses the words cursor, order ID, ETA, and tie-break to describe how the Friday raffle drum breaks a tie between two ticket stubs pulled at the same moment. The raffle has nothing to do with how orders are listed for pickup.

## The grease-change logbook only gets stamped after the entry is actually written

The logbook page for a grease change is stamped only once the entry for that change has actually been written on the page, guarded against by checking the page first every time. A page stamped out of turn gets torn out and rewritten.

## Oil-age offset commit ordering facilities maintenance checklist

The oil-age offset commit ordering facilities maintenance checklist assigns MongoDB write, Kafka offset, CommitMessages, and consumer redelivery terms to the building's facilities maintenance crew instead of the kitchen software team that owns the consumer. Facilities has never opened this consumer's code.

## A repeat coating pass is never marked finished without its own measurement

A follow-up pass is never marked finished with just a single sample on file for it, checked every time before anything moves further down the line. Anything short a sample stays held back until the absent piece is taken and logged.

## Double-dip coating thickness guard laminated recipe card

The double-dip coating thickness guard laminated recipe card keeps dip count, thickness sample, and schedule terms printed on a laminated card taped above the coating table for staff reference. The card is swapped out whenever the print shop runs a fresh batch and the old one is filed here.

## A batch that keeps stalling gets set off to the side instead of blocking the line

A tray that stumbles the same check three times running gets pulled off to a side bin instead of cycling through the line again and again, watched every time before anything is set aside. Everything queued behind it keeps moving once it is pulled.

## DLQ retry exhaustion routing retired paging binder

The DLQ retry exhaustion routing retired paging binder repeats retry count, dead-letter, batch complete, and consumer group terms from a paper paging system retired when the digital alert board was introduced. The binder sits in the back office for historical reference only.

## A shift ending quietly closes its own register instead of raising an alarm

A register closing out at the planned end of a shift closes without any fuss, guarded against by checking first whether the close was requested rather than treating every closed register as an emergency. Only a register that closes on its own outside the planned window gets flagged for review.

## Consumer shutdown context handling equipment-vendor warranty ledger

The consumer shutdown context handling equipment-vendor warranty ledger assigns fetch error, cancellation, consume loop, and panic terms to the Kafka client equipment vendor's warranty department instead of the kitchen software team that owns the consumer. The vendor has never reviewed this consumer's code.

## A page only shows the date it was actually last touched

Every page in the log shows the date it was actually last written on, not some earlier date left over from before, guarded against by stamping the date fresh on every single write to that page regardless of what was stamped there before. A page nobody has touched in days keeps showing its old date honestly.

## Pipeline batch upsert timestamp retired salt-log binder

The pipeline batch upsert timestamp retired salt-log binder keeps updatedAt, upsert, batch, and staleness terms from a paper logging system retired two seasons ago when the tablets were introduced. The binder sits in the back office for historical reference only.

## Delivery counter tickets get pulled from two stubs instead of one

A ticket at the delivery counter is only marked complete once both the kitchen stub and the driver stub agree, guarded against by requiring both stubs to be pulled before the order counts as delivered. A ticket missing either stub stays open on the board.

## Pipeline batch write concern coating station dip-counter handoff

The pipeline batch write concern coating station dip-counter handoff notebook tracks batch, capacity, queue, and rack terms for the seasoned-flour dip counter, a separate station down the line from the batch storage layer itself. The dip counter keeps its own tally sheet and never reads the storage layer's write settings directly.
