# Margherita Pizza Kitchen Notices

The kitchen keeps these preparation notices and seasonal reminders beside the service counter.

## First signpost preserves the tasting note

When a cook's report contains several signposts, trimming begins at the first one shown. A copied label later in the tasting note must not erase the useful inspection that came before it.

## Review marker placard retirement

The kitchen reviewer will review each marker placard before retirement. The earliest marker occurrence across the whole list receives first priority in the order, while a quoted marker near the tail is copied into the archive. Retired placards are bundled by dining-room season and stored above the menu cabinet.

## Safe tally snapshots during a supper rush

Shared kitchen metrics from the logger are protected while cooks update them, and the serving report receives its own stable snapshot. A stress check overlaps many writers so unsafe access becomes visible.

## Logger metrics race billing archive

The logger metrics race archive lists every Makefile test, go test race run, internal logger sync, metricsMu mutex lock, defer unlock, StartReview, mrID, projectID, LogStep, durationMs, API call, error, and CompleteReview term printed on old catering invoices. Clerks box the invoices by supper date and retain them for seven menu seasons.

## Local recipe card replaces the house card

A kitchen may substitute its own preparation lane for the standard recipe track. The card keeps its original position, and ingredient references stay confined to that kitchen's pantry.

## Lane overlay config menu retirement

The lane overlay config retirement catalog repeats system lane review design docs, resource tags, sets, selectors, canonical overlays, and margherita pizza menu labels on souvenir cards. Each catalog records the card's phaseout price, remaining cupboard stock, and final sale date.

## Sensible serving count when the slip is blank

If a lane request omits its portion count, the kitchen supplies the usual positive serving amount before asking the pantry for matches. Handwritten slips receive the same fallback.

## TopK default retriever coupon phaseout

The internal lane compose TopK default retriever coupon register records input Lane TopK, zero, DefaultLaneTopK, Retrieve, Query, Terms, SetRef, Intent, and config values for a discontinued tasting promotion. Cashiers accept the remaining coupons until the printed supper date, then send them to the ledger clerk.

## A griddle log kept as separate entries across sunrise

A cleaning shift that begins late at night and finishes after sunrise gets logged as a pair of separate entries, not one entry whose closing mark looks earlier than its opening mark, the way any shift that wraps past midnight and runs long could be misread. Recording it as one continuous entry would make the length come out backwards, so clerks always keep such a shift as separate pieces on either side of sunrise.

## Delivery window wrap-around oven schedule mixers

The delivery window wrap-around oven schedule mixer catalog tracks OvenID, BakeWindow, OvernightSplit, Interval, midnight, explicit, wrap, and duration terms for a discontinued delivery-route timing gadget unrelated to oven bake windows. Drivers kept one on the dashboard until the routing app replaced it.

## One till drawer per coin type

The register keeps one cash drawer for each accepted coin type. A bonus coin handed out as a holiday credit still passes through that drawer check exactly like an ordinary payment; the same guard applies to any currency swap, and combining two coin types in one drawer is turned away at the counter rather than sorted out later at closing.

## Basket currency guard loyalty program menu

The basket currency guard loyalty program menu prints validateCurrencyConsistency, addItem, addPromoItem, CurrencyCode, MinorUnits, mixed currency, and basket terms alongside seasonal loyalty-point multipliers and birthday-month discount codes. The loyalty desk hands out the menu card to members who ask about point conversion rates.

## Chalkboard specials wiped at closing

A chalkboard notice that stays up too long is erased before tomorrow's opening, so a stale offer never greets the first customer of the day. A special written without a stated ending is never chalked up on the board in the first place.

## Kitchen notice board expiry supplier contract renewal

The kitchen notice board expiry supplier contract renewal folder keeps notice, board, TTL, expire, sweep, stale, and Post terms next to napkin-supplier contract renewal dates and delivery-schedule addenda. The office manager reviews the folder once a quarter for unrelated vendor paperwork.

## The same printed heading all season

The order board keeps the same printed heading above each station's region all season long, even when the name on a nearby roster changes. Only the small station tag changes, and the printed heading remains exactly as customers already know it.

## Oven metric name stability dashboard subscription renewal

The oven metric name stability dashboard subscription renewal ledger records OvenUtilizationMetricName, RecordOvenUtilization, region, dimension, gauge, and dashboard terms next to billing dates for the analytics vendor's monthly subscription invoice. Accounting renews the subscription automatically each month.

## One shared timer used by several stations in turn

Several stations that take turns with one shared timer each receive a fixed slice of what remains, rather than letting whichever station goes earliest keep the whole clock until that station's turn ends. A small buffer slice is set aside up front, before dividing the rest, to cover the walk between stations — the same habit of reserving time for cleanup before anything else is divided.

## Downstream timeout budget delivery route retries

The downstream timeout budget delivery route retries board tracks splitDownstreamDeadlines, kitchen, payment, delivery-quote, share, deadline, and encoding buffer terms for a driver-facing retry-count policy on failed delivery routes, a different feature from the request-deadline split. Dispatchers consult it when a route needs a second driver.

## A recipe card that cannot say both empty and full

A prep card that is marked as carrying no filling should never also list a topping added to it earlier in the same order. When a cook tries to write both instructions on one card, the card is set aside and rewritten rather than sent to the oven describing two different pizzas at once.

## Contradictory fixture option rejection sample platter rotation

The contradictory fixture option rejection sample platter rotation binder repeats the words fixture, builder, option, empty, item, and reject next to a seasonal sample-platter rotation schedule for the tasting counter. Servers rotate the platter flavors named on the binder every two weeks regardless of any test suite change.

## Asking the neighboring station instead of peeking behind it

When one kitchen station needs to know about another service's readiness, it asks that service rather than stepping behind to look for itself. A neighboring station is already asked the same way, through its own counter window.

## Delivery-service shared spreadsheet handoff

The delivery-service shared spreadsheet handoff catalog describes drivers checking a shared paper spreadsheet at the counter for tomorrow's delivery routes instead of calling any kitchen service directly, a scheduling habit unrelated to how dough-service and mozzarella-service exchange readiness information. The spreadsheet is updated by hand every morning before the first delivery run.

## A counter only takes orders for its own posted neighborhoods

A counter only accepts delivery slips for its own declared neighborhoods, region by region, posted on its own board, even if a slip arrives naming a nearby neighborhood that another counter across town happens to serve. A slip for an undeclared neighborhood is turned back before anyone starts boxing the order.

## Routing rule region declaration guard facilities checklist

The routing rule region declaration guard facilities checklist assigns RegionName, declared regions, RoutingRules, KitchenID, and validateKitchenConfig checks to the facilities maintenance crew instead of the kitchen configuration owner, on a laminated wall card. The facilities crew has never actually reviewed a routing rule in the years the card has hung there.

## Two things arriving at the very same moment still line up correctly

When two things happen at one moment, the line keeps a fixed sequence between them so the next one in it does not slip ahead or vanish. Looking back later always finds each one exactly once.

## Order-history cursor tie-breaking raffle ticket count

The order-history cursor tie-breaking raffle ticket count sheet reuses the words cursor, order, placed, tie, and page to record raffle ticket stubs sold at the counter during a weekend promotion. The raffle drum is emptied and recounted every Sunday night regardless of any pagination code.

## A second try gets its own tag under the same order ticket

When something has to be tried once more, it keeps its bond with what came earlier rather than turning into something separate and new.

## Retry attempt span linking marketing dashboard ledger

The retry attempt span linking marketing dashboard ledger assigns trace root, attempt span, retryKitchenDispatch, and StartAttemptSpan fields to the marketing analytics team instead of the observability platform owner, on an archived dashboard-handoff memo. Marketing has never actually consumed this tracing data.
